# bf18-e4b-explore: cycle 4 data-collection variant of bf18-e4b

`bf18-e4b` with two changes: sampling temperature 0.7 (was 0.2) and the 30-minute calls-only clock of `bf18-calls`
(max_time_minutes 30; 28 calls, 80 turns, 240 s per call unchanged). Prompt, tools, model and thinking config unchanged.
Used only to collect E4B's own attempts on the training tasks for cycle 4 (DECISIONS 2026-11-04T06:32Z); its records
enter no comparison and no timing measure.
